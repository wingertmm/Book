# DEDICATION

---

*To those who understand that harmlessness is not virtue.*

*To be incapable of violence is not to be peaceful; it is merely to be harmless—a victim waiting on circumstance. Genuine morality requires the capacity to be formidable, paired with the absolute discipline to keep that power under voluntary control.*

*To the guardians, warriors, and practitioners who refuse the comfort of naive innocence; who carry the terrible capacity for force, yet shoulder the heavier burden of duty, restraint, and the defense of others.*

*And to the fighters who test their assumptions on the canvas, knowing that in the arena of physical violence, the truth is the test.*

---

*"In a world of wolves, the sheepdog must be dangerous."*
