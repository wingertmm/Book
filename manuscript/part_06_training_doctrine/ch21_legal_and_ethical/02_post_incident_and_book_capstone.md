## Section 21.2: Post-Incident Procedures: Medical, Exit, and Legal Preservations

The fight is not over when the attacker falls; scan 360 degrees, check your baseline, and secure your immediate perimeter. In the aftermath of violent combat, defenders frequently drop their guard, exhale in relief, and stand motionless above the collapsed threat. 

We designate this critical window as the **Golden Ten Minutes**. These six hundred seconds determine whether you survive secondary ambush attacks, bleed out from undetected wounds, or spend decades in prison.

The instant kinetic combat terminates, execute three mandatory perimeter actions:
1. **The 360-Degree Threat Scan**: Predators rarely operate alone (Chapter 20). Break tunnel vision immediately: scan for incoming associates, vehicle weapon retrievals, or gathering hostile crowds while maintaining your staggered base.
2. **Defensive Tool Containment**: If you deployed a legal firearm, blade, or impact tool, return it to safe retention or holster immediately once the threat stops. Standing over a fallen adversary with a drawn weapon when police cruisers arrive is suicidal; responding officers will perceive an armed individual as an active killer and engage.
3. **The Scene Exit Decision Matrix (Stay vs. Relocate)**: 
   - *When to Relocate*: If the confrontation occurred in hostile gang territory, outside a violent venue, or if accomplices regroup, execute tactical relocation. Move toward a well-lit, populated commercial sanctuary or police precinct while maintaining telephone contact with dispatch.
   - *When to Remain*: If the environment is secure, remain on scene. Unjustified flight creates an immediate legal presumption of guilt. Prosecutors routinely weaponize flight as direct proof of a guilty conscience.

### Stress-Induced Analgesia and Physical Self-Triage

Never assume you are uninjured because you feel no pain; adrenaline masks catastrophic trauma. During violent survival encounters, sympathetic flooding releases beta-endorphins and dynorphins, triggering **Stress-Induced Analgesia** and severing pain transmission through the spinal dorsal horn.

In close-quarters weapon entanglements (Chapter 20), victims universally report that they never felt penetrating blade wounds. They believed the assailant landed bare-knuckle punches, only to collapse minutes later from hypovolemic shock. With pain signaling muted, arterial lacerations remain unfelt until blood loss induces hypoxia.

Never rely on subjective feeling to assess your physical integrity. Execute the **Hands-On Blood Sweep Protocol** before dialing dispatch:
- Strip gloves or bulky clothing that impede tactile feedback.
- Vigorously sweep bare palms across your body in structural sequence: neck, carotid triangles, armpits, chest, abdomen, lumbar spine, groin, and thighs.
- After sweeping each quadrant, inspect your palms for bright arterial blood or dark venous pooling.
- Check boots and pant cuffs for saturation.

If your sweep reveals arterial bleeding, your operational priority shifts instantly to traumatic life support. Apply a combat tourniquet high and tight on the affected limb, pack junctional wounds with hemostatic gauze, or seal torso perforations with occlusive chest seals. You cannot mount a courtroom defense from a morgue slab.

### 911 Call Architecture and First Responder Management

Treat the 911 call as an unalterable courtroom transcript; state the facts, request medical aid, describe your appearance, and stop talking. The dispatch recording becomes State's Exhibit 1 in court, scrutinized word-for-word by prosecutors and jurors.

Panicked defenders destroy their cases by screaming emotional rants or uttering fatal phrases like "I had to teach him a lesson," handing prosecutors evidence of malice.

To safeguard your freedom, enforce the five-point **911 Call Architecture**:
1. **State Exact Location First**: "I am at Fourth Street and Elm, outside the public library." If the connection fails, dispatch knows where to deploy units.
2. **Request Dual Response**: "Send police and an ambulance immediately." Requesting medical aid shows you lacked malicious intent.
3. **Identify as the Victim and State Threat Neutralization**: "I was attacked by an individual. The attacker is down and requires medical attention." Establish yourself unambiguously as the victim of an unlawful assault.
4. **Provide Assailant Description**: "The attacker is a tall male in a dark green hooded jacket and jeans."
5. **Provide Your Description and Compliance Posture**: "I am wearing a navy jacket and grey pants. I am the victim, my hands are empty, and I am waiting near the entrance."

Deliver these five points and terminate the call or set the phone down. Never debate dispatch or discuss combat mechanics on an open line.

When police cruisers arrive, understand the officers' perspective. Responding officers enter an unverified scene with weapons drawn under intense stress:
- Spread empty hands wide at chest level, palms forward.
- Never hold a phone, flashlight, or defensive tool.
- Avoid sudden movements or running toward cruisers.
- Comply instantly with all commands to kneel, lie prone, or accept handcuffs. Handcuffs represent an investigative safety measure, not an admission of criminal guilt.

### The 24-Hour Rule and Cognitive Preservation

Never give a detailed formal interview during the adrenaline crash; preserve your rights, request counsel, and allow your memory to consolidate. Within fifteen to forty-five minutes of combat, the body undergoes severe autonomic collapse: the post-adrenaline crash.

As catecholamines deplete, the nervous system swings into parasympathetic exhaustion, producing tremors, nausea, cognitive fog, and severe temporal dilation. If asked to estimate strike counts or elapsed seconds, your statements will be wild, inaccurate guesses.

Major law enforcement agencies recognize this biological reality. Department policies mandate **The 24-Hour Rule**, barring officers from formal depositions until one or two full sleep cycles elapse. Sleep normalizes neurochemistry and allows the hippocampus to consolidate fragmented sensory inputs into accurate memory engrams.

As a civilian defender, protect yourself through **Cognitive Preservation**:
1. **Establish Core Ground Facts**: Confirm your identity, identify yourself as the defender, and point out transient evidence: *"His knife slid under that red vehicle; those two bystanders saw him charge me."*
2. **Deliver the Professional Invocation**: When investigators probe for a detailed blow-by-blow account, state firmly: *"Officer, I was attacked and forced to defend my life. I will cooperate fully, but I am in physical shock, I require medical evaluation, and I request my attorney present before answering detailed questions or providing a formal statement."*
3. **Maintain Absolute Silence**: Once you invoke your Fifth and Sixth Amendment constitutional rights, cease speaking. Do not joke with transporting officers, do not explain mechanics to hospital nurses, and do not discuss the incident on recorded phones. Silence protects your future.
