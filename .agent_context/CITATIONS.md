# Primary Citations & Strategic Lineage

Authoritative references backing the strategy, airpower doctrine, military ground maneuver, martial arts philosophy, and empirical neuroscience in *Time, Distance, and Power*.

---

## 1. Classical Military Strategy & Sun Tzu
- **Sun Tzu (trans. Griffith, S.B., 1963)**: *The Art of War*. Oxford University Press.
  - Primary citations: Ch. 1 (Estimates / Pre-calculation), Ch. 3 (Offensive Strategy / Subduing without fighting), Ch. 6 (Weak Points & Strong / Avoiding strength and striking weakness).
- **Clausewitz, Carl von (1832)**: *On War*. Princeton University Press. (Economy of force, friction, and center of gravity).

---

## 2. Airpower Doctrine & Decision Cycles
- **United States Air Force (2020)**: *Air Force Doctrine Publication 1 (AFDP 1): Airpower Operations*. Curtis E. LeMay Center for Doctrine Development and Education.
  - Principles cited: Speed, Range, Flexibility, Versatility, Concentration of Effects, Synergistic Effects, Priority, and Balance.
- **Boyd, John R. (1987)**: *A Discourse on Winning and Losing*. Air University Press.
  - Foundations for the OODA Loop (Observe-Orient-Decide-Act), fast-transient mental maneuvers, and decision-cycle disruption.
- **Warden, John A. III (1995)**: *The Enemy as a System*. Airpower Journal. (Parallel attack and strategic center of gravity).

---

## 3. Ground Maneuver & Army Combatives
- **Headquarters, Department of the Army (2017)**: *Field Manual 3-0 (FM 3-0): Operations*. Department of the Army.
  - Principles cited: Maneuver, Security, Objective, Mass, and Actions on Contact.
- **Headquarters, Department of the Army (2012)**: *Training Circular 3-25.150 (TC 3-25.150): Combatives*. Department of the Army.
  - Tactical rules: Body control, dominant angle, close the distance to negate striking or create distance to disengage.

## 4. Martial Arts & Tactical Philosophy
- **Lee, Bruce (1975)**: *Tao of Jeet Kune Do*. Ohara Publications. (Simplicity, directness, economy of motion, intercepting fist, "the man who has practiced one kick 10,000 times").
- **Kano, Jigoro (1932)**: *Mind Over Muscle: Writings from the Founder of Judo*. Kodansha. (Maximum efficiency with minimum effort / *Seiryoku Zenyo*; Mutual welfare and benefit).
- **Grossman, Dave & Christensen, Loren W. (2004)**: *On Combat: The Psychology and Physiology of Deadly Conflict in War and in Peace*. PPCT Research Publications. (Vasoconstriction, heart rate spikes, fine motor breakdown, and stress inoculation).
- **De Becker, Gavin (1997)**: *The Gift of Fear*. Little, Brown and Co. (Intuitive risk assessment, pre-incident indicators).

## 5. Empirical Neuroscience, Motor Learning & Tactical Stress

### Automaticity & Cortical-Subcortical Shift
- **Sanchez-Lopez, A., et al. (2014)**: *Differences in visuo-motor control in skilled vs. novice martial arts athletes during sustained and transient attention tasks: A motor-related cortical potential study*. **PLOS ONE**, 9(3), e91112. [DOI: 10.1371/journal.pone.0091112](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0091112).
  - *Annotation*: Proves skilled martial artists exhibit reduced prefrontal activation and faster, cue-facilitated motor automaticity. Supports the Command Center vs. Lizard Brain model (Ch 04).
- **Ashby, F. G., Turner, B. O., & Horvitz, J. C. (2010/2012)**: *Cortical and basal ganglia contributions to habit learning and automaticity*. **Trends in Cognitive Sciences / Neurocomputational Models**. [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S2212683X12000382).
  - *Annotation*: Details the neuro-anatomical transition from attention-demanding prefrontal cortical loops early in learning to automated sensorimotor loops (basal ganglia, motor cortex, cerebellum) after extended practice. Supports Chapter 04 and Chapter 19.
- **Du, Y., et al. (2022)**: *The relationship between habits and motor skills in humans*. **Trends in Cognitive Sciences**, 26(5), 371–387. [Cell Press](https://www.cell.com/trends/cognitive-sciences/abstract/S1364-6613(22)00038-9).
  - *Annotation*: Establishes that deliberate practice iteratively refines automatic behavioral components, freeing up cognitive capacity for high-level tactical and strategic decisions.

### Expert Kinematic Cue-Reading & Anticipation
- **Russo, G., & Ottoboni, G. (2019)**: *The perceptual-cognitive skills of combat sports athletes: A systematic review*. **Psychology of Sport and Exercise**, 44, 60–72. [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S146902921830668X).
  - *Annotation*: Demonstrates that expert martial artists outperform novices by extracting critical kinematic cues (weight distribution, hip rotation, eye line) earlier and more accurately from realistic stimuli. Directly anchors Section 4.4 and Chapter 03.
- **Halperin, I., Chapman, D. W., Martin, D. T., Lewthwaite, R., & Wulf, G. (2016)**: *Attentional focus effects on punch impact force and velocity in elite boxers*. **Journal of Sports Sciences / UNLV**. [UNLV Faculty Pub](https://gwulf.faculty.unlv.edu/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/Halperin_Wulf_2016_Blitz1.pdf).
  - *Annotation*: Demonstrates that an external focus (outcome/target) produces significantly higher velocity, impact force, and motor fluidity than an internal focus (conscious micromanagement of limbs). Validates why over-thinking ruins execution.

### High-Stress Physiology & Conscious Cognitive Collapse
- **Lieberman, H. R., Bathalon, G. P., Falco, C. M., Georgelis, J. H., Morgan, C. A. III, Niro, P., & Tharion, W. J. (2005)**: *Severe decrements in cognition functioning and mood induced by sleep-loss, heat, dehydration, and combat-simulation stress (The Fog of War)*. **US Army Research Institute of Environmental Medicine / DTIC**. [ADA454957](https://apps.dtic.mil/sti/citations/tr/ADA454957).
  - *Annotation*: Quantifies the severe breakdown of working memory, logical reasoning, reaction time, and vigilance under simulated combat stress, proving that complex conscious problem-solving fails under adrenaline.
- **Force Science Institute & Siddle, B. K. (1995/2020)**: *Sharpening the Warrior’s Edge: The Physiology of High-Stress Performance and Heart-Rate Arousal Thresholds*. **Force Science News / PPCT**. [Force Science](https://www.forcescience.com/?p=128369).
  - *Annotation*: Establishes operational physiological zones: fine motor deterioration begins above ~115 BPM, complex motor/analytical degradation above ~145 BPM, and sensory narrowing/tunnel vision above ~175 BPM. Explains why only deeply drilled gross-motor neural pathways survive.
- **Staal, M. A. (2004)**: *Stress, cognition, and human performance: A literature review and conceptual framework*. **NASA / Ames Research Center & PubMed Reviews**. [PMC8834678](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8834678/).
  - *Annotation*: Details how acute stress shifts brain processing from deliberative prefrontal control toward habitual subcortical responses, and demonstrates how stress inoculation training preserves performance under arousal. Anchors Chapter 18 and Chapter 19.
