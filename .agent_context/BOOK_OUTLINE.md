# Book Outline & Master Syllabus

**Title**: *Time, Distance, and Power: The Strategy, Physics, and Decision Framework of Self-Defense*  
**Subtitle**: *A Martial and Military Synthesis: Principles Over Techniques for Real-World Violence*  
**Author Persona**: Retired Air Force Officer, Multi-Discipline Martial Artist (Wrestling Coach, Judo, TKD Black Belt, Japanese Jujitsu Black Belt, BJJ Purple Belt).

---

## Pedagogical Architecture & Core Thesis
Technique manuals collapse under adrenaline. High-level performance in real violence relies on **subconscious neural pathways** formed through realistic training, governed by an immutable triad of physics: **Time, Distance, and Power**. By synthesizing Sun Tzu, Airpower doctrine (speed, concentration, decision-cycle advantage), and Army tactical maneuver (security of position, actions on contact), this book provides a comprehensive, principles-based strategic decision engine for self-defense.
